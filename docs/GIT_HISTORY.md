# Initial history provenance

This project's first 57 commits were reconstructed from a completed local
snapshot on October 18, 2026. At the owner's request, their author and committer
dates span October 2–18, excluding October 8, 11 and 15, with a +05:30 offset.
These chosen timestamps do not establish when development or testing occurred.

The commits group final files by responsibility; intermediate commits are not
independently validated builds. Private AI coordination files were subsequently
removed from every commit in the public branch. Their original commit positions
and messages remain, including positions whose remaining changes are empty.
Validation applies to the complete project snapshot. Future work uses actual
timestamps.
